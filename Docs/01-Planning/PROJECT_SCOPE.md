# Project Scope

**Document ID:** SC-PLN-003  
**Version:** 0.1

## In scope for the proposed MVP
1. Patient, doctor, administrator, and hospital-management access roles.
2. Patient and doctor profile management.
3. Patient appointment requests.
4. Doctor review, approval, or rejection of appointment requests.
5. Appointment cancellation and status notifications.
6. Doctor availability management.
7. Clinician-created post-discharge follow-up plans.
8. Patient recovery updates and follow-up history.
9. Email and SMS reminder integration (provider selection TBD).
10. Preliminary heart disease risk assessment.
11. Preliminary diabetes risk assessment.
12. Medicine packaging/strip image upload and OCR-assisted possible matching.
13. Audit events for sensitive actions.
14. Basic administrative and aggregate operational views.

## Out of scope for the initial MVP
- Autonomous diagnosis or treatment recommendations.
- Prescribing or dispensing medicines.
- Guaranteed identification of an individual tablet from appearance alone.
- Direct integration with live hospital information systems.
- Insurance claims and billing.
- Nationwide health-record interoperability.
- Production clinical use before appropriate validation and approvals.
- Advanced predictive analytics beyond the two agreed risk-assessment areas.

## Scope control
Any new feature must have:
- A user/problem justification.
- A documented requirement.
- An impact assessment for schedule, security, privacy, and architecture.
- Product-owner approval before implementation.
