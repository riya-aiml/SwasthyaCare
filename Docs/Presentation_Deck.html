02. Problem Statement

The Healthcare Continuity & Operational Breakdown

🚨 30-Day Post-Discharge 'Black Hole'

19%–23% of discharged hospital patients suffer an adverse event within 30 days. Over 60% are due to unmonitored medication titration or delayed reporting of deteriorating symptoms.

⚠️ High-Concurrency Double-Booking

Traditional check-then-act appointment systems allow multiple patients to book the same physician slot under concurrent peak traffic, causing clinical chaos and patient friction.

🧠 Clinician Diagnostic Cognitive Fatigue

Physicians have under 10 minutes per patient to manually evaluate multi-factorial cardiovascular (ASCVD) and diabetes (FINDRISC) risks during outpatient consultations.

💊 Pharmaceutical Packaging Confusion

Polypharmacy patients routinely misidentify generic medications due to manufacturer packaging variations, leading to accidental double-dosing or missed regimens.

🎙️ Speaker Note: Modern hospitals fail in the post-discharge recovery period. Between discharge and a 30-day visit, there is zero digital monitoring. In parallel, standard booking systems suffer from race conditions under high concurrent volume.
03. The SwasthyaCare Solution

An Integrated Clinical Intelligence Platform

1. Atomic Concurrency (CAS) Scheduling

Uses atomic Compare-And-Swap database operators (`findOneAndUpdate`) to guarantee absolute slot exclusivity with zero double-booking collisions (HTTP 409 Conflict mitigation).

2. 30-Day Follow-Up & Idempotent Reminders

Structured care plans authored by attending doctors; daily patient recovery check-ins (1–5 rating, vitals); automated SMS/Email queue with compound deduplication keys.

3. AI Decision Support (SaMD Boundary)

FastAPI microservice executing Framingham 10-Yr ASCVD Risk, FINDRISC 10-Yr Diabetes scoring, and medicine packaging OCR tokenization with mandatory clinical disclaimers.

4. HIPAA-Compliant Multi-Role Portals

Tailored consoles for Patients, Doctors, Management, and Admins. Executive analytics use database-level `$group` aggregations with zero patient PII exposure.

07. Deep Dive 1

Atomic Compare-And-Swap (CAS) Concurrency

❌ Naive Anti-Pattern (Vulnerable) 
const slot = await Slot.findById(id);
if (slot.status === 'available') {
  // Concurrency window here!
  // Competing request also reads 'available'
  slot.status = 'reserved';
  await slot.save(); // Double booking!
}

Two requests execute concurrently, both see 'available', both book.

✅ SwasthyaCare Atomic CAS Pattern 
const slot = await Slot.findOneAndUpdate(
  { _id: id, status: 'available', isBooked: false },
  { $set: { status: 'reserved', isBooked: true } },
  { new: true }
);
if (!slot) return res.status(409).json({
  message: 'Slot reserved by another patient'
});

Database executes check and update as one atomic CPU operation.

Empirical Test Verification:

In tests/sprint8.test.js, parallel booking promises execute simultaneously via Promise.all(). Exactly one receives HTTP 201 Created; the competing request receives HTTP 409 Conflict. Race condition probability: 0.00%.

08. Deep Dive 2

Post-Discharge Care & Idempotent Reminders

1. Doctor Authors Plan

Attending physician specifies primary diagnosis, follow-up duration (14–30 days), frequency, and medication titration schedule upon hospital discharge.

2. Patient Daily Check-In

Patient submits daily recovery rating (1–5 scale), blood pressure, heart rate, and symptom updates. Doctors receive instant triage alerts for low scores.

3. Idempotent Queue

Scheduled reminders use compound deterministic hash MD5(planId + date + channel) with a MongoDB unique index, guaranteeing zero duplicate alerts.

🩺 Clinical Impact: Directly mitigates the 19%–23% 30-day post-discharge complication rate by providing structured physician oversight during the most vulnerable recovery window.
09. Deep Dive 3

AI Clinical Decision Support (SaMD Compliance)

Framingham ASCVD Risk

Sex-stratified Cox regression evaluating 10-year risk of heart attack or stroke using age, cholesterol, SBP, smoking, and diabetes.

Risk Bands: Low (<5%), Inter (7.5-19.9%), High (≥20%)
FINDRISC Diabetes Score

Validated 0–26 additive scoring matrix evaluating 10-year probability of Type-2 Diabetes based on BMI, waist, diet, and family history.

Risk: 1% (<7 pts) to 50% (>20 pts)
Medicine OCR Matcher

Cleanses packaging image text and computes Levenshtein fuzzy distance against institutional formulary monographs.

Confidence threshold: 0.75 for verified match
⚖️ FDA SaMD Medical Boundary: Every payload carries a mandatory clinical disclaimer. The microservice operates strictly for decision support and risk stratification; it explicitly does not dispense autonomous medical prescriptions.
