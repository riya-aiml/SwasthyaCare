## Requirement quality checklist
For each requirement, verify:
- It describes one capability.
- It has a unique ID.
- It uses clear, testable wording.
- Its actor and permissions are clear.
- It has acceptance criteria or a linked test.
- It does not conflict with another requirement.
- It has an agreed priority.
