# ICMR & Indian Guidelines on Hypertension-IV

## 1. Classification of Blood Pressure for Adults
Based on clinic blood pressure measurements taken after 5 minutes of seated rest:
- **Normal:** Systolic $< 120$ mmHg and Diastolic $< 80$ mmHg.
- **Pre-hypertension:** Systolic $120 - 139$ mmHg or Diastolic $80 - 89$ mmHg.
- **Stage 1 Hypertension:** Systolic $140 - 159$ mmHg or Diastolic $90 - 99$ mmHg.
- **Stage 2 Hypertension:** Systolic $\ge 160$ mmHg or Diastolic $\ge 100$ mmHg.
- **Hypertensive Crisis (Red Flag):** Systolic $> 180$ mmHg and/or Diastolic $> 120$ mmHg with chest pain, visual changes, or neurological deficit requires immediate emergency hospitalization.

## 2. Non-Pharmacological Lifestyle Interventions
- **Dietary Sodium Restriction:** Total dietary sodium intake should not exceed $2,000$ mg/day (equivalent to approximately 5 grams or 1 flat teaspoon of table salt per day). Avoid heavily salted pickles, processed papads, packaged snacks, and instant noodles.
- **Potassium-Rich Foods:** Adequate potassium intake (3,500–5,000 mg/day) from fresh fruits (bananas, oranges), green vegetables, and coconut water aids blood pressure reduction, provided renal function is normal.
- **Alcohol and Tobacco:** Complete tobacco cessation (smoking and smokeless chewing tobacco) and avoidance of alcohol consumption.
- **Body Weight Optimization:** Aim for a target BMI between $18.5 - 22.9$ kg/m² and waist circumference $< 90$ cm for men and $< 80$ cm for women per Asian-Indian anthropometric cut-offs.
