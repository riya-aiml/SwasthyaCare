# Clinical Guideline: ICMR Guidelines for Management of Hypertension
**Source ID:** ICMR-HTN-2022  
**Institution:** Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR)  
**Publication Year:** 2022  
**Target Scope:** Scope B (Clinical Public Health Knowledge)  

## 1. Blood Pressure Classification for Adults
* **Optimal Blood Pressure:** Systolic $< 120$ mmHg and Diastolic $< 80$ mmHg.
* **Prehypertension / Elevated BP:** Systolic $120\text{--}139$ mmHg or Diastolic $80\text{--}89$ mmHg.
* **Stage 1 Hypertension:** Systolic $140\text{--}159$ mmHg or Diastolic $90\text{--}99$ mmHg.
* **Stage 2 Hypertension:** Systolic $\ge 160$ mmHg or Diastolic $\ge 100$ mmHg.
* **Hypertensive Urgency / Crisis:** Systolic $> 180$ mmHg or Diastolic $> 120$ mmHg without target organ damage requires urgent medical evaluation. If accompanied by chest pain, shortness of breath, or neurological deficits, it is an emergency.

## 2. Dietary Sodium & Potassium Guidance
* **Sodium Restriction:** Total dietary sodium intake should be limited to $< 2.0$ grams per day (equivalent to $< 5.0$ grams of table salt, approximately 1 level teaspoon per day).
* **Processed Foods:** Avoid packaged pickles, papads, commercial salted snacks, and instant noodles which contain high hidden sodium.
* **Potassium Enhancement:** Increase dietary potassium through fresh fruits (bananas, oranges), green vegetables, and coconut water (unless renal impairment is present).

## 3. Lifestyle Modifications
* **Weight Management:** Maintain BMI between $18.5$ and $22.9$ kg/m² for Indian adults.
* **Aerobic Exercise:** 30 to 45 minutes of daily moderate aerobic exercise (brisk walking) reduces systolic BP by 4 to 9 mmHg.
* **Stress Management:** Incorporate yoga and diaphragmatic deep breathing exercises.
