# Clinical Guideline: WHO Guidelines on Cardiovascular Disease Prevention
**Source ID:** WHO-CVD-2021  
**Institution:** World Health Organization (WHO)  
**Publication Year:** 2021  
**Target Scope:** Scope B (Clinical Public Health Knowledge)  

## 1. Major Modifiable Cardiovascular Risk Factors
* **Tobacco Use:** Total cessation of all forms of smoked and smokeless tobacco. Tobacco use accelerates coronary atherosclerosis and endothelial dysfunction.
* **Dyslipidemia:** Target levels for cardiovascular health:
  * Total Cholesterol: $< 200$ mg/dL ($< 5.2$ mmol/L).
  * LDL Cholesterol: $< 100$ mg/dL ($< 2.6$ mmol/L) for primary prevention; $< 70$ mg/dL for high-risk patients.
  * HDL Cholesterol: $> 40$ mg/dL for men, $> 50$ mg/dL for women.
  * Triglycerides: $< 150$ mg/dL ($< 1.7$ mmol/L).

## 2. Dietary Pattern (Mediterranean & Heart-Healthy Eating)
* Replace saturated and industrial trans fats with unsaturated fatty acids (mustard oil, olive oil, sunflower oil, nuts, seeds).
* Consume at least 400 grams (5 portions) of non-starchy fruits and vegetables daily.
* Restrict free sugars to less than $10\%$ of total caloric intake.

## 3. Physical Activity Benchmarks
* Adults aged 18–64 should engage in at least 150–300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic exercise, or 75–150 minutes of vigorous-intensity aerobic physical activity weekly.
* Muscle-strengthening activities involving major muscle groups on 2 or more days per week provide additional metabolic benefit.
