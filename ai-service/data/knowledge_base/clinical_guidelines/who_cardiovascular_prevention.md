# WHO Guidelines for Cardiovascular Disease Prevention

## 1. Key Modifiable Cardiovascular Risk Factors
Cardiovascular disease (CVD) is the leading cause of premature mortality globally. Major modifiable factors include:
- Tobacco smoke exposure (both active smoking and second-hand smoke).
- Dyslipidemia: elevated low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C) and elevated triglycerides.
- Uncontrolled hypertension and diabetes mellitus.
- Physical inactivity and high-saturated-fat/trans-fat dietary patterns.

## 2. Prevention and Target Milestones
- **Smoking Cessation:** Complete cessation of tobacco products within 1–2 years reduces coronary heart disease risk by 50%.
- **Dietary Lipids:** Saturated fat should be limited to $< 10\%$ of total caloric intake; industrial trans-fats must be completely eliminated. Replace with monounsaturated and polyunsaturated fatty acids from nuts, seeds, and vegetable oils.
- **Physical Activity:** Adults should perform 150–300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic physical activity, or 75–150 minutes of vigorous-intensity aerobic physical activity per week.
