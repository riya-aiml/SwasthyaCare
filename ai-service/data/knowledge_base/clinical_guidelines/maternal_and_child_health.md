# Maternal and Child Health Care Guidelines

## 1. Antenatal Care (ANC) Schedule
Per Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) and World Health Organization (WHO) standards, every pregnant woman must receive comprehensive antenatal care:
- **Minimum 4 Antenatal Visits:**
  - 1st Visit: Within the first trimester (before 12 weeks of gestation) for registration, dating ultrasound, and baseline blood tests (Hb, ABO-Rh, VDRL, HIV, Hepatitis B, blood sugar).
  - 2nd Visit: Between 14 and 26 weeks (anomaly scan, gestational diabetes screening).
  - 3rd Visit: At 28–32 weeks (growth monitoring, BP screening for pre-eclampsia).
  - 4th Visit: At 36 weeks until term (fetal presentation, birth preparedness, delivery planning).
- **Nutritional Supplementation:**
  - Daily Iron and Folic Acid (IFA) tablets (100 mg elemental iron and 500 mcg folic acid) for at least 180 days during pregnancy.
  - Calcium supplementation (1,000 mg/day) starting from 14 weeks onwards to prevent pregnancy-induced hypertension.
  - Two doses of Tetanus and adult Diphtheria (Td) vaccine or a single booster if vaccinated within the past 3 years.

## 2. Maternal Emergency Danger Signs
Immediate emergency hospital care is required if any of the following obstetric red flags occur:
- Vaginal bleeding at any stage of pregnancy.
- Severe persistent headache, blurred vision, or epigastric pain (signs of pre-eclampsia / eclampsia).
- Convulsions or loss of consciousness.
- High fever with chills or foul-smelling vaginal discharge.
- Sudden gush of fluid from vagina (premature rupture of membranes).
- Noticeable reduction or cessation of fetal movements in the third trimester.
- *Emergency Ambulance Services: Call **102** (Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram - JSSK free transport) or **108** for critical emergency evacuation.*

## 3. National Immunization Schedule (NIS) for Infants
Essential early childhood vaccines administered under the Universal Immunization Programme (UIP):
- **At Birth:**
  - BCG (Bacille Calmette-Guérin) for tuberculosis prevention.
  - Hepatitis B birth dose (within 24 hours).
  - OPV (Oral Polio Vaccine) zero dose.
- **At 6 Weeks, 10 Weeks, and 14 Weeks:**
  - Pentavalent vaccine (Diphtheria, Pertussis, Tetanus, Hepatitis B, and Hib).
  - Fractional Inactivated Poliovirus Vaccine (fIPV).
  - Rotavirus Vaccine (oral drops for diarrheal prevention).
  - Pneumococcal Conjugate Vaccine (PCV - doses at 6 weeks, 14 weeks, and booster at 9 months).
- **At 9–12 Months:**
  - Measles & Rubella (MR) 1st dose.
  - Vitamin A 1st dose (100,000 IU).

## 4. Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF)
- **Early Initiation:** Initiate breastfeeding within one hour of birth (colostrum must be given; pre-lacteal feeds like honey, water, or ghutti must be avoided).
- **Exclusive Breastfeeding:** Exclusive breastfeeding is recommended for the first 6 months of life. No additional water, teas, or animal milk should be introduced before 180 days.
- **Complementary Feeding:** From 6 months onward, introduce clean, nutritious, age-appropriate complementary foods (mashed lentils/dal, boiled vegetables, rice porridge, mashed banana) while continuing breastfeeding up to 2 years of age or beyond.
