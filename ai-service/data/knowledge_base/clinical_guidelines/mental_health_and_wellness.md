# NIMHANS & WHO Clinical Guidelines for Mental Health and Emotional Wellness
**Document ID:** NIMHANS-MH-2023
**Scope:** Clinical Guidelines — Scope B
**Governing Bodies:** National Institute of Mental Health and Neurosciences (NIMHANS) & World Health Organization (WHO)

---

## 1. Acute Anxiety and Panic Attack Management
Anxiety is characterized by persistent worry, physiological arousal (racing heart, shallow breathing, muscle tension), and feelings of dread. Panic attacks involve sudden surges of intense fear reaching a peak within minutes.
* **4-7-8 Breathing Technique:** Inhale through the nose for 4 seconds, hold breath for 7 seconds, and exhale slowly through mouth for 8 seconds. This stimulates the vagus nerve and activates the parasympathetic nervous system.
* **5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding Technique:** Acknowledge 5 things you can see, 4 things you can touch, 3 things you can hear, 2 things you can smell, and 1 thing you can taste. This anchors awareness in the present environment.
* **Reassurance:** Remember that panic symptoms, while intensely uncomfortable, are not dangerous and will naturally subside.

---

## 2. Chronic Stress, Burnout and Emotional Overwhelm
Prolonged stress elevates cortisol levels, disrupts sleep, and compromises immune resilience.
* **Cognitive Pacing:** Break overwhelming tasks into small, achievable increments.
* **Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR):** Systematically tense and release major muscle groups from toes to forehead.
* **Digital Boundaries:** Limit continuous exposure to distressing news and work notifications, especially 60 minutes before bedtime.

---

## 3. Sleep Hygiene and Insomnia Management
Adequate restorative sleep (7–9 hours for adults) is foundational to emotional regulation and metabolic health.
* Maintain a consistent sleep-wake schedule, even on weekends.
* Avoid caffeine, heavy meals, and vigorous physical exertion within 4 hours of sleeping.
* Keep the sleeping environment dark, quiet, and cool ($18\text{--}22^\circ\text{C}$).
* If unable to sleep after 20 minutes, leave the bed, engage in a low-light calming activity (reading), and return only when sleepy.

---

## 4. Professional Clinical Support & Crisis Resources
When feelings of anxiety, sadness, or hopelessness persist for more than two weeks, impair daily functioning, or lead to social withdrawal, professional evaluation by a clinical psychologist or psychiatrist is strongly recommended.
* **India National Mental Health Helpline (Tele-MANAS):** Dial **14416** or **1800-891-4416** (24/7 free, confidential multilingual counseling).
* **Emergency Services:** If there is immediate risk of self-harm, call **112** or visit the nearest hospital emergency department.
