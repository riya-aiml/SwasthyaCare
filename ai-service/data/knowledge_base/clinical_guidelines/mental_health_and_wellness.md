# Mental Health, Stress Resilience & Crisis Support

## 1. Non-Pharmacological Stress Reduction Techniques
- **Mindfulness & Diaphragmatic Breathing:** Engaging in 10–15 minutes of slow abdominal breathing (4-second inhale, 6-second exhale) reduces sympathetic arousal, lowers heart rate variability stress, and mitigates acute anxiety.
- **Cognitive Reframing:** Identifying cognitive distortions (catastrophizing, black-and-white thinking) and grounding oneself in present sensory observations.
- **Physical Movement:** Moderate daily exercise directly stimulates endorphin production and neurotrophic factor (BDNF) release, counteracting depressive symptoms.

## 2. Emergency Mental Health Crisis & Escalation Contacts
If an individual experiences severe psychological distress, feelings of hopelessness, or thoughts of self-harm or suicide, non-urgent conversational systems must immediately escalate:
- **Tele-MANAS (Government of India):** Toll-free 24/7 mental health crisis helpline: **14416** or **1800-891-4416**. Available across 20+ Indian regional languages.
- **KIRAN Helpline:** **1800-599-0019** (Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment).
- **Vandrevala Foundation:** **+91 9999 666 555** (24/7 mental health counseling).
- **Emergency Medical Response:** **112** or **108**.
