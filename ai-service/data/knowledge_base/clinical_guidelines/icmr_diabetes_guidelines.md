# Clinical Guideline: ICMR Guidelines for Management of Type 2 Diabetes
**Source ID:** ICMR-DIA-2023  
**Institution:** Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR)  
**Publication Year:** 2023  
**Target Scope:** Scope B (Clinical Public Health Knowledge)  

## 1. Diagnostic Thresholds & Glycemic Targets
* **Normal Fasting Plasma Glucose:** $< 100$ mg/dL ($< 5.6$ mmol/L).
* **Impaired Fasting Glucose (Prediabetes):** $100\text{--}125$ mg/dL ($5.6\text{--}6.9$ mmol/L).
* **Diabetes Diagnostic Cutoff:** Fasting plasma glucose $\ge 126$ mg/dL ($\ge 7.0$ mmol/L) on two separate occasions, or 2-hour post-75g oral glucose load $\ge 200$ mg/dL ($\ge 11.1$ mmol/L).
* **Glycated Hemoglobin (HbA1c):**
  * Normal: $< 5.7\%$.
  * Prediabetes: $5.7\%\text{--}6.4\%$.
  * Diabetes: $\ge 6.5\%$.
  * Therapeutic Target for Most Non-Pregnant Adults: $< 7.0\%$.

## 2. South Asian BMI & Waist Circumference Cutoffs
* Due to higher percentage of visceral adipose tissue at lower body weights in South Asian populations, ICMR defines:
  * Normal BMI: $18.5\text{--}22.9$ kg/m².
  * Overweight: $23.0\text{--}24.9$ kg/m².
  * Obesity: $\ge 25.0$ kg/m².
* Abdominal obesity action limits: Waist circumference $\ge 90$ cm for South Asian men and $\ge 80$ cm for South Asian women.

## 3. Lifestyle & Non-Pharmacological Interventions
* **Physical Activity:** At least 150 minutes per week of moderate-intensity aerobic physical activity (e.g., brisk walking, cycling), distributed over at least 3-5 days per week with no more than 2 consecutive days without activity.
* **Dietary Fiber:** High-fiber complex carbohydrates (whole grains, legumes, pulses, green leafy vegetables) should comprise 50-60% of daily caloric intake.
* **Refined Sugars:** Simple sugars, sweetened beverages, and refined carbohydrates should be minimized to prevent glycemic spikes.
