# ICMR Guidelines for Type 2 Diabetes Management

## 1. Diagnostic Thresholds and Screening Criteria
According to the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR):
- **Normal Glycemia:** Fasting Plasma Glucose (FPG) $< 100$ mg/dL; HbA1c $< 5.7\%$.
- **Impaired Fasting Glucose (Pre-diabetes):** FPG $100 - 125$ mg/dL; HbA1c $5.7\% - 6.4\%$.
- **Provisional Diabetes:** FPG $\ge 126$ mg/dL; 2-hour postprandial glucose $\ge 200$ mg/dL; or HbA1c $\ge 6.5\%$ (confirmed on a repeat test in asymptomatic individuals).
- Routine screening is recommended starting at age 30 for South Asian individuals, or earlier in the presence of risk factors (central adiposity, BMI $> 23$ kg/m², first-degree family history).

## 2. Medical Nutrition and Dietary Recommendations
- **Carbohydrate Quality:** Complex carbohydrates with high soluble dietary fiber (whole grains, oats, brown rice, pulses) should constitute 50–55% of total caloric intake. Highly refined flours (maida) and sugar-sweetened beverages must be restricted.
- **Dietary Fiber:** Aim for at least 30–40 grams of fiber per day from legumes, fresh green leafy vegetables, fenugreek (methi), and whole fruits.
- **Portion Control:** The Indian plate method recommends dividing the plate into 50% non-starchy vegetables, 25% lean protein/pulses, and 25% whole grains.

## 3. Physical Activity Standards
- At least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic physical activity per week (such as brisk walking at 5 km/h, cycling, or swimming), spread over at least 5 days with no more than 2 consecutive days without exercise.
- Resistance training (bodyweight exercises, light resistance bands) 2–3 times per week to preserve insulin sensitivity and muscle mass.
