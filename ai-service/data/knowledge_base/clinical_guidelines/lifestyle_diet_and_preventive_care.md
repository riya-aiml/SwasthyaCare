# ICMR & NIN Dietary Guidelines and Lifestyle Wellness
**Document ID:** ICMR-NUTR-2024
**Scope:** Clinical Guidelines — Scope B
**Governing Bodies:** Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) & National Institute of Nutrition (NIN)

---

## 1. Balanced Dietary Principles for South Asian Adults
* **Macronutrient Balance:** Source at least 50–55% of daily energy from unrefined complex carbohydrates (millets, brown rice, whole wheat), 15–20% from diverse protein sources (lentils, paneer, soy, eggs, fish), and $< 30\%$ from healthy dietary fats.
* **Fiber Intake:** Aim for at least 30–40 grams of dietary fiber daily from green vegetables, whole legumes, and seasonal fruits.
* **Sodium & Added Sugar Restrictions:** Restrict salt intake to $< 5$g/day ($< 2$g sodium) and limit added refined sugars to $< 25$g/day.

---

## 2. Physical Activity & Metabolic Health
* **Aerobic Exercise:** At least 150–300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic exercise (brisk walking, cycling, swimming) or 75 minutes of vigorous exercise per week.
* **Resistance & Flexibility:** Engage in strength training 2 days per week to preserve muscle mass and optimize insulin sensitivity.
* **Sedentary Breaks:** Interrupt prolonged sitting every 30 to 45 minutes with a 2-minute posture break or gentle walk.
