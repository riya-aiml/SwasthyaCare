# Lifestyle, Dietary & Preventive Care Guidelines

## 1. Principles of Balanced Nutrition
- **Dietary Diversity:** Consume a diverse variety of whole grains, legumes, pulses, seasonal colorful vegetables, whole fruits, nuts, and dairy products.
- **DASH Dietary Pattern:** The Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension (DASH) diet stresses generous portions of fruits, vegetables, low-fat dairy, whole grains, and lean proteins while restricting added sugars and saturated fats.
- **Hydration:** Consume 2.5 to 3 liters of clean drinking water daily, adjusting for ambient humidity and physical exertion. Avoid sweetened packaged juices and carbonated beverages.

## 2. Restorative Sleep and Circadian Hygiene
- Adults require 7 to 9 hours of uninterrupted sleep per night. Consistent sleep and wake times stabilize neuroendocrine cortisol rhythms and autonomic blood pressure regulation.
- Minimize screen exposure to blue light within 60 minutes before bedtime.
