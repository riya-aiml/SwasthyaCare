# ICMR Clinical Guidance for General Outpatient Symptoms & First-Aid Triage
**Document ID:** ICMR-GEN-2023
**Scope:** Clinical Guidelines — Scope B
**Governing Body:** Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR)

---

## 1. Fever and Viral Infection Supportive Care
Fever ($\ge 100.4^\circ\text{F} / 38^\circ\text{C}$) is an adaptive immune response to infectious agents.
* **Supportive Care:** Maintain liberal fluid intake (oral rehydration solutions, coconut water, warm broths) and prioritize physical rest.
* **Monitoring:** Record body temperature every 4 to 6 hours.
* **Red Flags:** Seek prompt physician consultation if fever exceeds $103^\circ\text{F}$, persists beyond 3 days, or is accompanied by stiff neck, shortness of breath, confusion, or rash.

---

## 2. Headaches and Tension Management
Headaches are frequently provoked by dehydration, prolonged screen glare, muscle tension, lack of sleep, or sinus inflammation.
* **Immediate Comfort Measures:** Rest in a quiet, dimly lit room. Apply a cold or warm compress to forehead or temples. Ensure adequate hydration.
* **Red Flags (Thunderclap / Secondary):** Sudden explosive onset ("worst headache of life"), focal neurological weakness, or onset after head trauma requires emergency evaluation.

---

## 3. Gastrointestinal Discomfort: Acidity, Indigestion & Bloating
Dyspepsia and gastroesophageal reflux are commonly triggered by spicy or high-fat foods, late-night eating, irregular meal timings, and stress.
* **Comfort Measures:** Consume smaller, frequent meals. Avoid lying flat for at least 2 hours after eating. Stay hydrated with water or cooling buttermilk.
* **Red Flags:** Recurrent vomiting, black tarry stools, difficulty swallowing, or severe localized abdominal tenderness warrant medical investigation.

---

## 4. Respiratory Symptoms: Cough and Sore Throat
Upper respiratory viral infections are self-limiting in the majority of immunocompetent individuals.
* **Comfort Measures:** Steam inhalation once or twice daily, warm water gargles with a pinch of salt, and warm honey-lemon water (for individuals over 1 year of age).
* **Red Flags:** Persistent chest pain, difficulty catching breath, coughing up blood, or oxygen saturation ($SpO_2$) $< 94\%$ requires immediate clinical assessment.
